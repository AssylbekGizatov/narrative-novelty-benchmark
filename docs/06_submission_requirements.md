# DiGRA submission and layout check

Checked 15 November 2026 against primary guidance.

## Regional submission

[DiGRA Central Asia Conference 2027 — official CFP](https://digra.org/digra-central-asia-conference-2027-call-for-papers/) specifies an English abstract of 150–300 words covering research question, novelty/contribution, methodology, and results or preliminary findings. Deadline: 23 November 2026, 23:59 UTC+5. Notification: 18 January 2027. Conference: 27 February 2027, School 21, Tashkent. Accepted authors are later invited to submit an extended abstract or paper; abstract acceptance alone does not guarantee proceedings publication.

The separate `paper/conference_abstract.txt` follows that regional abstract length and contains completed computational results. Author/contact details are separate. The manuscript's shorter abstract follows the general paper-template guidance. No submission was made on the author's behalf.

## Manuscript layout

[DiGRA chapter digital-library guidance](https://digra.org/digra-digital-library-chapter-information/) points chapter submissions toward the current DiGRA template. The reference used here is the official [DiGRA 2026 Word template](https://assets.zyrosite.com/m7VD52w3knSxENxZ/digra-2026-submission-template-mP432Vykj8ty192Z.docx).

| Requirement / choice | Implementation |
|---|---|
| User's maximum length | Ten pages including references in the named PDF; anonymous copy checked separately. |
| Page | A4, single column. |
| Written template margins | 3.50 cm left/right, 1.70 cm top/bottom. |
| Margin inconsistency | The downloaded template's stored section dimensions differ from its written instructions. This draft follows the explicit written values. |
| Body typography | Open Sans 10 pt, the template's stated alternative; justified, single spacing. |
| Heading typography | Arial 12 pt; level-one headings uppercase and bold. |
| Title | Arial 18 pt, bold and centred. |
| Figure/table captions | 11 pt bold; kept with their object. |
| Citations | Author–date; bibliography with primary URLs/DOIs. |
| Equations | Editable native Word math, not screenshots. |
| PDF size | Below the template's 4 MB threshold. |
| Review copy | Separate anonymous DOCX/PDF; author name and affiliation removed from its body and author metadata. |
| Publication status | Research draft; no proceedings masthead, fabricated acceptance or DOI. |

The international DiGRA 2027 full-paper track's 5,000–7,000-word limit is a different requirement. This compact paper follows the requested maximum of ten pages and the regional abstract-first route. It should not be described as automatically compliant with every DiGRA event or with later, not-yet-issued regional full-paper instructions.

Before external submission, the named author should verify affiliation wording, disclose AI assistance according to the venue's then-current policy, and check the invitation-specific format. These are normal author responsibilities, not evidence of completed human-subject approval or peer review.
