# Source appraisal and claim boundaries

Research date: 15 November 2026. This is a targeted literature investigation supporting an original computational study, not a systematic review or a numerical result about a count of publications. Searches followed several independent lines: AI-assisted writing and collective diversity; plot recurrence; interactive-narrative authoring and player responsiveness; scientific ideation; choice theory; metamorphic testing. Primary papers, author-hosted copies, official repositories and conference guidance were preferred. No inaccessible paper is represented as having been read in full.

## What transfers to this project, and what does not

| Primary source | Useful finding or design precedent | Limitation on transfer | Consequence for our study |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Doshi and Hauser 2024](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.adn5290), *Generative AI enhances individual creativity but reduces the collective diversity of novel content* | Individual story ratings and collective similarity can move differently under AI assistance. | Short-story ideation is not executable game authoring; its effect size is not a game-narrative estimate. | Separate within-version properties from diversity across authors; do not import the published effect size. |
| [Padmakumar and He 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.05196), *Does Writing with Language Models Reduce Content Diversity?* | Co-writing and different model training regimes motivate workflow-specific diversity comparisons. | Content diversity depends on task and assistance condition; it is not a universal law of all AI use. | A future trial must log the model version, assistance mode, prompts and accepted suggestions. |
| [Xu et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2504966122), *Echoes in AI*; [authors' project](https://echoes.msr-emergence.com/) | Plot-level recurrence can persist beyond surface wording; continuation comparisons can detect this. | Recurrence of narrative events does not identify how player actions reach them. Automated judgments have their own validity assumptions. | Evaluate conditional action–response relations, while treating semantic event extraction as a separate unresolved layer. |
| [Si, Yang and Hashimoto 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.04109), *Can LLMs Generate Novel Research Ideas?* | Expert-rated novelty of individual ideas and diversity across generated ideas are separate questions. | Scientific ideation has different constraints, expert communities and evaluation targets from games. | Use the distinction conceptually; do not claim evidence that AI cannot abduct or that game creativity must decline. |
| [Lu, Zhou and Wang 2025](https://doi.org/10.1145/3706598.3713363), *WhatELSE*; [author-hosted paper](https://www.research.autodesk.com/app/uploads/2025/02/WhatELSE.pdf) | Prior interactive-storytelling work already studies narrative-space control, plot diversity and effects of contrasting player actions. | A system's authoring evaluation and this measurement audit are different research objects. | Withdraw any claim that this project is the first interaction-aware narrative benchmark. Narrow the contribution to controlled measurement failure modes and alignment/coverage checks. |
| [Sun et al. 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.09099v1), *Drama Llama* | Storylet authoring can expose responsiveness and author assessments including originality. | The preprint's system and participant evaluation are not our data; its results cannot be relabelled as our findings. | Position this study alongside existing authorable-responsiveness work; retain separate structural and human-perception outcomes. |
| [Calderwood, Wardrip-Fruin and Mateas 2022](https://computationalcreativity.net/iccc22/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/ICCC-2022_2L_Calderwood-et-al..pdf), *Spinning Coherent Interactive Fiction through Foundation Model Prompts* | Spindle demonstrates mixed-initiative generation across multiple passages. | Generating coherent passages is not proof of preserving an author's unusual branching structure. | Compare executable versions and provenance, not only final prose. |
| [Mawhorter et al. 2014](https://www.cs.hmc.edu/~pmawhorter/research/papers/towards_a_theory_of_choice_poetics-Mawhorter_Mateas_Wardrip-Fruin_Jhala-2014.pdf), *Towards a Theory of Choice Poetics* | Choice framing, options, outcomes and interpretation matter together. | A consequence count cannot substitute for the expressive or aesthetic value of a choice. | Do not equate branch reduction with lower quality, lower agency or lower originality. |
| [Tikhonov 2024](https://aclanthology.org/2024.games-1.8/), *Branching Narratives: Character Decision Points Detection* | Identifying consequential character decisions connects NLP to branching narrative. | Decision-point detection is not alignment between two executable versions. | Cite the adjacent task without claiming it solves our correspondence problem. |
| [Lee, Liang and Yang 2022](https://coauthor.stanford.edu/), *CoAuthor* | Interaction logs record how writers accept, reject and revise assistance. | Logs from other writing tasks cannot establish the provenance of a new game's design. | The proposed authoring study retains suggestions and edits, not just a final story. |
| [Segura et al. 2016](https://doi.org/10.1109/TSE.2016.2532875), *A Survey on Metamorphic Testing* | Known transformations allow testing when a full output oracle is unavailable. | Passing a transformation test establishes an implemented property, not validity of a creativity construct. | Label perfect aligned-pair detection as a definitional check; add counterexamples, stronger baselines and failure cases. |
| [inkle/ink-library](https://github.com/inkle/ink-library), pinned source commit in the data manifest | Executable, MIT-licensed narrative materials with creator attribution. | A two-story convenience corpus does not represent commercial games, all genres or all cultures. | Report source provenance, errors, finite sampling and the exact runtime. |
| [inkjs](https://github.com/y-lohse/inkjs), version 2.3.2 | Officially compatible compiler/runtime implementation exposes real saved-state execution. | Runtime projection is still partial; hidden future distinctions may survive equal immediate observations. | Execute actual programs and replay successors; avoid an unsound regex-only graph parser. |

## Search and reasoning record

Representative query families used during research included `generative AI creative writing diversity`, `LLM scientific novelty human research ideas`, `AI interactive narrative originality benchmark`, `WhatELSE narrative diversity player actions`, `Drama Llama originality`, `choice poetics outcomes`, `Ink open source stories license`, and `DiGRA Central Asia 2027 abstract requirements`. Searches were expanded when an apparent novelty claim was contradicted by prior interaction-aware systems. This record is not a claim of exhaustive indexing or a preregistered search protocol.

The Einstein/Solovine opening suggested in the initial brief was not adopted. It would require verification of the particular letter and a careful philosophical argument about induction, deduction and abduction. The assertion that current LLMs categorically lack abduction is not established by this project's experiments. The paper instead begins with a problem that its own data and design address: differently worded endings need not create additional possibilities of play.

Industry posts can motivate a problem, but no studio anecdote or social-media claim is used as quantitative evidence. Claims about model collapse under recursive training are also distinct from within-session human–AI co-authoring and were not imported as an explanation for our measurements.

## Claims deliberately rejected

- “We are the first to evaluate interactive narrative originality”: too broad given WhatELSE, Drama Llama and related work.
- “AI reduces game originality by a measured percentage”: no authoring treatment was executed.
- “Graph metrics outperform text metrics”: the paired prose baseline challenges this.
- “100% sensitivity proves originality detection”: sensitivity follows from the aligned-pair eligibility rule.
- “9,341 samples provide industry-wide statistical power”: mutations share only 234 contexts from two games.
- “Loss of endings equals loss of agency”: this confuses a projection with player interpretation.

The resulting contribution is an original executable audit of measurement assumptions, with disclosed negative and qualifying results.
